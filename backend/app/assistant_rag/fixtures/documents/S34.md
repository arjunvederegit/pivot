## Primary creation: Mateo Cruz leads

Ilan Marchetti averages 5.6 assists per game, much of it from the second side rather than as the initiator. ## Secondary creation: Marchetti's complementary game
Ilan Marchetti is the better second-side and secondary option next to a ball-dominant guard. Marchetti attacks closeouts, makes the extra pass and runs second-side pick-and-roll without needing to start the possession. Mateo Cruz can also create from either side, but his game is built around having the ball; paired with another ball-dominant guard, Mateo Cruz would give up some of what makes him valuable, and that pairing is untested.

Marchetti gets screened off the ball handler and is also targeted, but his size and team-defense anticipation make him easier to hide. Neither guard should be a team's primary on-ball defender.
