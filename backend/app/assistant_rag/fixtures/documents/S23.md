# Omaha Riverhawks — rebuild timeline and targets

*Kenji Mori, Riverhawks front office (fictional) · 2026-01-22 · team strategy memo*

The Omaha Riverhawks are rebuilding, and this memo sets the front office's timeline and target profile. We prefer players aged 24 or younger with real development upside, our top need is a young lead creator, and we are willing to move veterans whose timeline does not match ours. Patience matters more to the Riverhawks than a quick fix.

## Riverhawks timeline

The Omaha Riverhawks are not built for this season's playoffs and will not pretend to be. Our timeline is a multi-year build, measured by how much our young players improve rather than by this season's record. That shapes every target: we prefer players aged 24 or younger, with room left to grow, who will still be improving when the core is ready to compete. A proven veteran who helps us win a few more games now does not move that timeline.

## Top need: a young lead creator

The Riverhawks' top need is a young lead creator: a guard who can run the offense, read the pick-and-roll, collapse the defense and create for others. We do not have that player on the roster. We will accept a creator who is still learning to defend; effort and positioning can be coached, but vision and pick-and-roll feel are harder to teach. We would rather wait for the right young creator than trade for an older one.

## Lucas Achterberg and the Riverhawks core

Lucas Achterberg, 22, is part of the Riverhawks' young core and is not available. A 7-0 center who anchors our drop coverage, Achterberg gives any lead creator we add a natural pick-and-roll partner. His foot speed in space is a development priority, and our staff will keep working on it. Any target should fit around Achterberg's timeline, not compete with it.

## Veterans, availability and patience

The Riverhawks are willing to move veterans whose timeline does not match the rebuild, such as Wes Calder, 33. A contender may value Calder's experience more than we can. Availability matters for development: young players improve by playing, so we prefer targets who stay on the floor. Above all, the Riverhawks front office will choose patience over quick fixes and will not spend future assets to chase short-term wins. Contract questions are handled outside this memo.
