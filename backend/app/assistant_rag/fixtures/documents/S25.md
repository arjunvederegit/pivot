# Savannah Tides — size without losing pace

*Abe Lindqvist, Tides front office (fictional) · 2026-01-27 · team strategy memo*

The Savannah Tides play pace-and-space small ball, and opponents are beating us on the glass and at the rim. This memo sets the front office's target: rim protection, rebounding and size, added without slowing the pace that defines the Tides. A non-shooting center is acceptable only if he protects the rim.

## Tides identity: pace and space

The Savannah Tides are a pace-and-space small-ball team. We push the ball after makes and misses, spread the floor with shooters and trust our guards and wings to create against a defense that is not set. That identity is not up for negotiation in this memo. Any big man the Tides add has to run the floor, get back on defense and keep the offense moving rather than slowing it down to play through the post.

## Where the Tides are losing

The Tides are losing possessions on the glass and points at the rim. Bigger opponents win the offensive boards against our small lineups and turn misses into second chances. Without a real deterrent, drivers attack the basket at will, and teams with size post up our wings when they want a reliable shot. The longer a game stays in the half court, the more those possessions add up against the Tides.

## What the Tides need in a big

The Tides front office wants three things: rim protection, rebounding and size. The ideal target is a mobile big who protects the rim, finishes defensive possessions with a rebound and can keep up with our pace. Shooting is welcome but secondary. A non-shooting center is acceptable only if he protects the rim; a big who neither shoots nor protects the rim does not fit the Tides.

## Tides roster context

Ilan Marchetti and Amari Fenwick are already on the Tides roster and are not trade targets. Marchetti's playmaking would benefit from a big who screens well and rolls hard, and Fenwick's development as a young wing is part of our longer-term plan. Any frontcourt addition should make life easier for Marchetti and Fenwick, not take their minutes. Contract questions are handled outside this memo.
