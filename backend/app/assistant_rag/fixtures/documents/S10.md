## Rim protection

Dante Cruz is an elite rim deterrent who averages 2.3 blocks per game, and on film the blocks understate his effect: drivers pull up short, change angles or kick the ball back out rather than challenge him at the basket. He contests vertically and is at his best protecting the paint from a drop position. Film shows too few possessions of Dante Cruz switched onto guards to judge that part of his game. Rim protection: A.

## Rebounding

Dante Cruz is a strong defensive rebounder who averages 10.9 rebounds per game, and most of that work comes on the defensive glass, where he finds a body early, seals and secures the ball with two hands. He is less of a factor on the offensive glass, partly because the Sundogs often send him back on defense, and that keeps the grade short of the top mark. Rebounding: A-.

## Spacing and offensive role

On that volume there is no meaningful three-point percentage, and this report deliberately does not quote one. The cost shows up on film: the defender assigned to Dante Cruz sags into the paint, which crowds the driving lanes for the Sundogs' guards and lets help defenders load up early. On offense he is a screener, roller and dump-off finisher. Spacing: D.
