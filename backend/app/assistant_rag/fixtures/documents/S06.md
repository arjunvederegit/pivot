# Theo Vance — early-season screen-navigation concerns

*Marcus Ibe, Analytics scout (fictional) · 2025-11-14 · scouting report*

This note flags one specific concern about Theo Vance, the 25-year-old, 6-6 wing for the Bayou Herons: his screen navigation at the point of attack. It is based on nine games of film from late October and early November. Point-of-attack defense: C+. It is an early read on a small sample and should be re-checked later in the season.

## Screen navigation

On film, Theo Vance died on screens. In nine games, ball handlers who used a high screen against Vance routinely got him on their back: he went under too often, got caught on the screener's hip and conceded downhill drives that forced his big to step up and left the roller open. Quick guards noticed. Several opponents hunted Vance in ball screens late in games, calling for his man to set the screen. Point-of-attack defense: C+.

## Against wings

The concern about Theo Vance is narrow. When he guards a wing his size away from the ball or in isolation, he is positionally fine: he stays between his man and the basket, contests and does not gamble. This note does not change our view of Vance as a positional defender, and it does not grade that part of his game. The problem is specifically the possession that starts with a ball screen.

## Sample caveat

Nine games of film is a small sample for Theo Vance, and early-season film carries its own noise: rotations, conditioning and new schemes all settle over the first weeks. The season's statistics are too early to quote, and box-score figures would not capture screen navigation anyway. We recommend re-checking Vance's screen navigation later in the season on a larger sample before this grade is used in any trade evaluation.
