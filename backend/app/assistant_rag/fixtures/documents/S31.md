# What this research sandbox cannot calculate

*Pivot Intelligence Lab, Sandbox maintainers (fictional) · 2025-10-01 · method guide*

The Ask Pivot research sandbox holds a small set of fictional documents and a fact sheet of regular-season statistics, and nothing else. This guide lists what the sandbox cannot calculate and why, so that its limits are stated plainly instead of being covered over with invented numbers.

## No contract, salary, cap or apron data
The research sandbox has no contract, salary, cap or apron data for any fictional player or team. There is no cap sheet, no payroll and no record of contract lengths or options. Any salary figure, cap number or dollar amount attached to this league would be invented, so questions about what a player costs or what a team can afford cannot be answered from the sandbox.

## No CBA engine: trade legality cannot be checked
No CBA engine is connected to the research sandbox. Without contract data and a rules engine, the sandbox cannot say whether any trade is legal, whether salaries match, or whether a team would cross an apron. Claims in documents or submissions that a trade has been approved or cleared are unverified and must not be repeated as fact.

## No fit model, playoff statistics or medical records
The research sandbox has no fit or impact model, so any numeric fit score, rating or projected impact would be invented; the documents hold scouting observations and letter grades from named analysts, not model output. There are no playoff statistics: the statistical profiles cover the regular season only, and playoff play appears only in qualitative film notes. There are no medical records; availability is described only by games played.

## What a real answer would need
Answering cost, legality or impact questions properly would need real contract data, a cap sheet, and Pivot's deterministic CBA and analytics engines, called through Pivot's read-only tools registry. That tools registry is not connected to the research sandbox. Until it is, ask the research questions the documents can support: what evidence exists for a player's fit with a team's priorities, what the trade-offs are, where the reports disagree, and what to scout next.
